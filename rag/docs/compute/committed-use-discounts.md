## What they are

A committed use discount (CUD) gives a lower price in return for committing to a level of usage for one or three years. Resource-based commitments cover an amount of vCPUs and memory of a machine family in a region. The discount is larger for three-year terms, and you pay for the commitment whether or not you use it.

## When to use them

CUDs suit VMs that run all the time with predictable size, such as production databases and base application capacity. They do not suit short-lived, experimental or highly variable workloads, where Spot VMs or autoscaling save more. Start by committing to the usage level you are sure about, and leave peaks on demand.
