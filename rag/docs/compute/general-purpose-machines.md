## E2 compared with N2 and N1

E2 machine types are the lowest-cost general-purpose family and suit web servers, small databases, development environments and other workloads without special CPU needs. N2 and N2D offer higher and more consistent per-core performance and larger shapes at a higher price. N1 is an older generation that is rarely the best value for new workloads.

## Shared-core types

The e2-micro, e2-small and e2-medium shared-core types are the cheapest option and suit small or bursty workloads that do not need a full vCPU all the time. Moving a workload that does not need N2 performance to an E2 type of the same size lowers its price without changing its vCPU or memory.
